# 大小寫回歸批（EN）——ASR 與手機輸入法的產物型態
#
# 為何獨立成一批：系統裡 21 個英文詞表**全是小寫**，而
#   ① whisper 一律輸出首字大寫＋專有名詞大寫
#   ② 手機輸入法（iOS 預設）自動大寫首字
#   ③ 訪客開 CapsLock / shift 沒放開（blUetooth）
# 打字訪客用電腦不會打大寫，所以 r1-r5 五輪收斂**一句都沒覆蓋**。
#
# ⚠️ 最危險的是**寫入路徑**（F 段）：確認詞大小寫敏感 ⇒ 訪客講
#   `Go Ahead` 以為確認了、其實沒寫入（小寫同句是真的會寫入的）。

### K1 商品名大寫
> how many Bluetooth Earphones left | inventory_single | Wireless Bluetooth Earphones
> stock of Yoga Mat | inventory_single | Yoga Mat
> Wireless Mouse count | inventory_single | Wireless Mouse
> do we have Power Bank | inventory_single | Power Bank

### K2 句中動詞/倉名大寫（iOS 自動大寫首字）
> North received 50 wireless mouse | movement_confirm | Wireless Mouse
> south shipped 30 Yoga Mat | movement_confirm | Yoga Mat
> Transfer 20 bluetooth earphones from north to Central | transfer_confirm
> Set safety stock for Yoga Mat to 80 | config_confirm

### K3 全大寫（CapsLock）
> HOW MANY BLUETOOTH EARPHONES LEFT | inventory_single | Wireless Bluetooth Earphones
> WHATS RUNNING LOW | low_stock
> NORTH RECEIVED 50 WIRELESS MOUSE | movement_confirm | Wireless Mouse

### K4 單字內亂大寫（shift 沒放開）＋ 錯字
> blUetooth earphones stock | inventory_single | Wireless Bluetooth Earphones
> yOga mat stock | inventory_single | Yoga Mat
> pOwer bAnk stock | inventory_single | Power Bank
> blUetoth earphones | inventory_single,clarify | Wireless Bluetooth Earphones
> wireles mOuse | inventory_single,clarify | Wireless Mouse
> WhAtS RuNnInG LoW | low_stock

### K5 意圖詞大寫（詞表比對點，最容易漏）
> What Else Do Coffee Beans Buyers Get | related,clarify
> Which Items Are Running Low | low_stock
> Why Is The Toothbrush Count Off | agent_rca
> Compare North And South | compare_warehouses
> Alert Me When Yoga Mat Drops Below 30 | alert_confirm

### K6 卡片確認詞大寫（🚨 寫入路徑——r5-voice 修，原本不會寫入）
> north received 10 steam iron | movement_confirm
> Go Ahead | movement_done
### K7 全大寫確認
> north received 10 steam iron | movement_confirm

### K8 肯定＋客套（原本連長度門檻都過不了，8 字元是中文調的）

### K9 同上大寫

### K10 alert 卡裸 confirm（原本回「No item matching "confirm"」）

### K11 alert 卡大寫

### K13 取消詞大寫變體
> south shipped 20 yoga mat | movement_confirm

### K14 新增商品分步流程：大寫輸入 + 大寫取消

### K15 🚨 不可被代按的（判錯＝直接寫入，代價最高）
! 疑問句該走引導、否定句該走取消，都**不可**代按
> north received 10 steam iron | movement_confirm
> is that all good | movement_confirm,clarify
### K16 否定

> no dont do it | movement_cancelled,item_cancelled,clarify,movement_confirm
